RoomHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

RoomHub is an iOS app developed by Fatih Emre Derici that helps roommates share expenses and chores. Contact: fatihemrederici22@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Optional profile and house photos you choose to upload.
  • Content you create: houses, expenses, payments and chores.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in and to show your shared house data to the roommates in your house. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your data

Your name, photo and house data are visible only to members of the same house. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Exchange rates are loaded from open.er-api.com; no personal data is sent to that service.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your profile and removes you from your house. Shared expenses and payments that involve other roommates stay in their house history (with your name) so their balances remain correct. If you are the last member of a house, the house and all its data are deleted. You can also request deletion by email.

Children

RoomHub is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and shown in the app.
